Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At KVORA AUTOMATIONS, accessible from our online platforms, protecting visitor and client privacy is fundamental to our operations. This policy details how we handle information collected through our web tools and services.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order or interact with our tools (including custom website setup, dashboard builds, and automated generators), we may request:

  • Contact information (Name, Email Address, Phone/WhatsApp number)
  • Business metadata necessary to customize invoices, quotation templates, or dashboards
  • Payment details processed securely via certified payment gateways

2. How We Use Your Information

We process collected data solely to deliver and optimize our services, specifically to:

  • Deliver custom websites, dashboards, and automated generation tools
  • Send order updates, system notifications, and customer support messages
  • Prevent fraudulent transactions and maintain platform security

3. Data Protection & Third Parties

KVORA AUTOMATIONS does not sell, lease, or rent client data to third parties. Data is shared strictly with trusted payment gateways and infrastructure service providers necessary to complete your requests.

4. Cookies & Web Analytics

We use standard cookies and analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to assess website traffic performance and improve user interaction across our tools.

5. Contact Information

If you have questions about this policy, contact us via email at support@kvora.com.
